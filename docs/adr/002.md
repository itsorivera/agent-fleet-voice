## ADR 002: Conversational Assistant for Consultative Wine Sales (B2C E-Commerce)

### Status
**Accepted**

### Context
A virtual sommelier is required to handle calls or web/mobile channels to advise, pair, and sell wines to end customers.
* **Key Non-Functional Constraints:**
* High demand for conversational fluency, warmth, intonation, prosody, and natural support for interruptions (*barge-in*).
* High reasoning ability and conversational empathy (capturing ambiguous preferences such as: "something not too dry for an Italian dinner").
* Interaction with the catalog (e-commerce) and payment gateway (generating links or sending them via SMS/WhatsApp).
* Minor errors or deviations do not pose systemic legal or financial risks.

### Decision
Adopt the **Realtime API** architecture (native Speech-to-Speech integrated with in-session *Tool Calling* / *Function Calling*).

### Technical and Business Justification
1. **Prosody, Tone, and Emotional Empathy:** The direct audio multimodal model grasps not only the words, but also the user's tone, hesitations, and pace, responding with human-like vocal inflections that boost conversion rates in high-value consultative sales.
2. **Ultra-Low Latency and Natural Interruptions:** Latency hovers around 350ms - 500ms. If the customer interrupts by saying "no, sorry, I actually prefer white wine," the model instantly stops acoustic synthesis without needing complex external orchestrators for echo cancellation and audio cutoff.
3. **Fluid Conversational Context:** Maintaining a unified multimodal session preserves conversational flow without the friction of complex re-prompting. Inventory lookups occur organically via native *tool-calling* ("check_wine_stock", "create_payment_link").

### Discarded Alternatives
* **Chained Voice Pipeline:** Dismissed because the cumulative latency disrupts the dynamic of a casual, charismatic sales conversation. Customer nuances (hesitations, excitement) are lost when converting audio to plain text ($$STT$$).
* **GPT-Live:** Although viable, it introduces unnecessary orchestration complexity for a use case where product catalogs and carts can be handled smoothly using the direct tools supported by the Realtime API.

### Consequences
* **Positive:** Maximum sales conversion driven by conversational intimacy; user experience indistinguishable from a human assistant; simplified client architecture (a single bidirectional socket).
* **Negative / Challenges:** Significantly higher operational cost per minute compared to traditional pipelines; not recommended for handling credit card data directly (mitigated by offloading payment to an external link sent via SMS or an out-of-band secure gateway).
