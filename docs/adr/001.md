## ADR 001: Contact Center for Regulated Banking Entity (5 Transactional Flows)

### Status
**Accepted**

### Context
Implementation of a voice bot is required for a banking contact center supporting 5 transactional and support workflows (e.g., fraud-related card blocking, balance inquiry, installment rescheduling, filing complaints/PQR, and step-up authentication).
* **Key Non-Functional Constraints:**
* Strict regulatory compliance (PCI-DSS v4.0, local banking/supervisory regulations, PII/NPI masking).
* Non-repudiation, comprehensive auditing at the exact verbatim transcription level, and deterministic guardrails (zero tolerance for hallucinations in amounts or transactions).
* Integration with a legacy Core Banking system via secure internal proxies.

### Decision
Adopt the **Chained Voice Pipeline** architecture ($$STT \rightarrow Middleware/PII \rightarrow Core/LLM \rightarrow Guardrails \rightarrow TTS$$) implemented modularly using streaming (*chunked transfer*).

### Technical and Business Justification
1. **Intermediate Inspection and Security Injection:** Unlike a unified *Speech-to-Speech* connection, the chained pipeline allows interposing a sanitization and sensitive entity detection layer (PII Redaction) before text touches the LLM or external analytics services, mitigating leakage of card numbers, CVVs, or passwords.
2. **Deterministic Control and Finite Guardrails:** In regulated banking workflows, critical transactions cannot be left to open generative discretion. This architecture allows the use of finite-state machines (FSM) or semantic routers that route critical intents to deterministic transactional APIs without passing through the generative layer if unnecessary.
3. **Auditability and Forensic Traceability:** Produces clear, decoupled artifacts: raw incoming audio, exact verbatim transcription (STT log), enriched prompt, core banking response, generated response, and output audio. This facilitates compliance audit requirements.
4. **Resilience and Vendor Independence:** If the STT or TTS provider degrades its SLA, it can be swapped via a *Circuit Breaker* without altering business logic or the reasoning model.

### Discarded Alternatives
* **Realtime API:** Dismissed due to being a "black box" during direct acoustic ingestion. It does not allow native interception for PII sanitization between user audio and model reasoning. High cost during prolonged on-hold sessions and risk of hallucinations in debit/credit operations.
* **GPT-Live:** Although it allows a decoupled backend, the continuous audio negotiation layer complicates PCI certification by streaming raw, potentially sensitive audio data to third-party clouds without intermediate inspection of textual payloads.

### Consequences
* **Positive:** Total security control, granular per-component observability, cost optimization on repetitive flows (ability to cache TTS responses for identical queries).
* **Negative / Challenges:** Higher aggregate latency (*End-to-End Latency* typically between 800ms - 1300ms vs. ~400ms in native speech-to-speech). Requires orchestrating *Audio Streaming* infrastructure (WebSockets, precise VAD, *barge-in* handling at the orchestrator level).
